Asks to decision-makers

What we ask of municipality, county and capital

  • 1 Acknowledge the 25 arealinnspill as a structured portfolio, not 25 isolated cases.
  • 2 Adopt the 10 work packages as the steering unit for municipal and county dialogue.
  • 3 Prioritise near-term pilots (housing at Rovdas; Sandnes Fjord destination; first service node).
  • 4 Keep large mountain/harbour concepts on a study track before major CAPEX.
  • 5 Invite state, county and private capital into a transparent public–private sequencing discussion.
