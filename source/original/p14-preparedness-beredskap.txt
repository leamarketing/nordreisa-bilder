                <h2>Preparedness system: Kvænnes–Nordkjosnes–Storvik</h2>
                <p>Internal PALT planning graphics for AP07 sketch a linked tunnel and facility system connecting three points: a deep-water quay and preparedness harbour, a mountain facility at Nordkjosnes, and an operational preparedness hub at Kvænnes. The figures below are draft planning estimates read from those graphics, not confirmed engineering figures — exact lengths vary slightly between drafts.</p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Dypvannskai og beredskapshavn</strong> (deep-water quay and preparedness harbour) — linked by a secure branch tunnel to the Nordkjosnes mountain facility.</li>

                    <li><strong>Hovedtunnel E6–Nordkjosnes</strong> (main tunnel): approx. 4.5 km (draft figures: 4.54 km / 4.47 km).</li>
